SolidWorks Orchestral Chessboard

A chess set where every piece is an orchestral instrument.

Role
Solo Project - GE 1502: Cornerstone of Engineering 2
Professor
Dr. Duncan Davis
Tools
SolidWorks
Timeline
March 2025

Overview

A themed chessboard where each chess piece is modeled after an orchestral instrument (or component of one), merging my background in classical music with 3D modeling skills. The project required designing six unique part files, a full board assembly, exploded views, and dimensioned drawing files.

Piece Design Mappings
Piece Instrument Key SolidWorks Techniques
Pawn Trumpet Mouthpiece Loft, fillet, draft, 3-pt arc, shell, revolved boss base
Knight Bassoon Sweep, spline, mirror, polygon
Bishop Harp Fillet, cut extrude
Rook Violin Spline, draft
King Trumpet Valves 3-pt arc, mirror entities, fillet
Queen Keyboard Mirror entities, fillet

Deliverables

The final submission included the full chessboard assembly, individual part files for all six pieces, exploded assembly views, and dimensioned engineering drawings for the board and a featured piece (keyboard).
